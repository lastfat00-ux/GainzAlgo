## 2026-05-18 - Enhancing Contributor UX
**Learning:** A structured "Visuals" comparison in PR templates reduces cognitive load for reviewers and ensures visual changes are intentional and accessible.
**Action:** Implemented a Before/After comparison table in the PULL_REQUEST_TEMPLATE.md.

## 2026-05-19 - Motion Sensitivity in Trading Apps
**Learning:** Financial dashboards often use high-frequency updates and animations (e.g., ticking prices) that can trigger vestibular issues; explicit reduced motion standards are critical for these contexts.
**Action:** Integrated `prefers-reduced-motion` requirements into the project's UX standards and PR checklist.
