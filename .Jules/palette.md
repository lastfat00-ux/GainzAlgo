## 2026-04-30 - Accessible Documentation Foundations
**Learning:** When encountering an empty repository, the primary micro-UX improvement is creating accessible documentation (README) and standardized contribution templates (.github/PULL_REQUEST_TEMPLATE.md) to provide context and reduce barriers for users and contributors.
**Action:** Use explicit HTML anchors for major headings in README.md to ensure reliable internal navigation, and wrap emojis in `<span role="img" aria-label="...">` to ensure they are accessible to screen readers.
